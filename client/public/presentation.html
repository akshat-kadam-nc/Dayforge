The problem

To-do lists hide the math.

A list that looks achievable, an illusion of free time, and work that spills over — because nothing ever counted the hours against a real budget.

🫥

Invisible time

📈

Silent overflow

Plans look fine until 9pm. Nothing warned you the day was already full.

🤷

No feedback loop

Estimates never get tested against reality, so they never improve.

The core idea

Your day is a budget, not a list.

  • 1Set your routine once. Sleep, commute, work hours and working days are subtracted to compute the time you really have.
  • 2Every task carries an estimate. A live timer logs real minutes against it as you work.
  • 3The cockpit shows allocated vs available, flags overflow the moment your plan exceeds the budget, and tracks planned vs actual.

Calendar events, chores and interruptions all draw from the same budget — so the number you see is the truth, not a wish.

How to use it

Up and running in four steps.

  • 1Set your routine — sleep, commute, work, working days. That's what makes 24 hours into your real available time.
  • 2Add tasks with estimates — pick the area, a function track, a deadline, and (optionally) link a weekly goal.
  • 3Run the day from Today — start timers, plan on the timeline, log interruptions, tick off chores.
  • 4Review in Reports — see where time went and tighten your estimates for next week.

Tip: try "Explore in demo mode" on the login screen for a fully populated sample day.
