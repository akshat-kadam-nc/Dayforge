# Dayforge — User Guide

A page-by-page how-to. Navigation is the left rail (collapses to a hamburger on
mobile): **Today · Calendar · Goals · Team · Reports · Settings**.

New here? Try **"Explore in demo mode"** on the login screen — it loads a rich
sample day with no account and nothing saved.

---

## First-time setup

1. **Register / log in** on the login screen.
2. **Set your routine** (Settings → Daily routine, or the onboarding prompt):
   sleep, commute, work minutes, and which days are working days. This is what
   turns a flat 24 hours into your real available time. Until you set it, the day
   is treated as fully open (24h).
3. (Optional) **Connect Google Calendar** (Settings) so meetings count against
   your budget.
4. (Optional) **Pick a wallpaper** (Settings → Wallpaper).

---

## Today — the cockpit

Your daily command center. Top to bottom:

- **Time Budget** — Available, Allocated, and Interrupted minutes. Toggle
  **Day / Week / Month**. The "time gained vs plan" line shows whether you're
  beating or trailing your estimates today.
- **24-Hour Allocation** — a ring of where the day's time is going, with a
  "Today by area" bar breakdown.
- **Day plan** — open it to get an hour-by-hour timeline. Drag a task to move it,
  drag its edge to resize (15-min snaps). Click an unscheduled task to drop it
  into the first free slot. Overlaps flag red.

**Working a task**
- Each task shows its estimate and logged time. Press ⚡ to start its timer
  (multiple can run at once), ⏸ to pause (time is kept), or ⏹ to stop and
  complete. You can also complete with the logged time, the allocated estimate,
  or a custom amount.
- **Create** a task with the ＋ button: title, area/venture, function track,
  estimate, start day, weekly goal link, and a soft/hard deadline.
- **Edit** any task from its ⋯ menu.

**Buckets**
- **Pending** — tasks from earlier days still open (overdue / carried over).
- **Scheduled** — tasks set for a future day. "Move to today" pulls one forward.

**Chores** — small 5/10/15-min end-of-day items. They batch into one timed
**Chores** block (right rail) ticked off a checklist; undone ones carry forward
with a ↩ badge.

**Interruptions** — log what derailed you in one tap: 🔥 Fire, 🌀 Rabbit Hole,
😵 Distraction. They show as their own slice of the day.

**Completed today** — a collapsible ledger of what you finished, with logged vs
plan. Each row has **↩ Reverse** (reopen it to your active list) and **✎** to
correct the logged time.

## Calendar

Day / Week / Month views of your tasks and synced events.

- **Month** — a grid tinted by the dominant life area each day, with event chips,
  a per-area allocation bar, and an overflow flag when a day is over budget.
- **Week** — a 7-day column breakdown.
- **Day** — a read-only detail: completed-task history (logged vs estimate),
  events, and planned tasks.
- Navigate with the header arrows or ← / → / `t` (today). Google events appear
  here and deduct from your budget; mute a recurring series you don't want
  counted.

## Goals

A hierarchy: **Annual → Half-year → Monthly → Weekly**, grouped by life area.

- **Progress is derived from the tasks you link to a goal**, so it reflects real
  work, not a slider you nudge.
- **Count goals** track completed linked tasks toward a target (e.g. "ship 3
  posts"). **Timed goals** carry a hard deadline and are marked **missed** if the
  deadline passes under target; other goals can complete late.
- **Conclude** closes any goal at a chosen percentage; **Reopen** reverts it;
  **Duplicate** spins up the next period's copy (progress reset, deadline shifted)
  for recurring goals.
- Terminal goals move into a collapsed **Closed** section (Done / Missed).
- Link a task to a weekly goal when you create it (Today ＋), and the goal's
  progress updates automatically.

## Team

Track work you've delegated to your people.

- **By Person** — an attention-sorted rail; pick someone to see their open items,
  status counts, and history. Change a delegation's status inline.
- **By Status** — a board across Pending / In progress / Done / Blocked.
- **Follow-ups due** strip surfaces what needs chasing. Filter by overdue /
  blocked / this week, or search.
- Add a report (person) and assign work via the modals.

## Reports

Look back and learn. Two tabs:

**Statistics** — pick a range (This week / month / Last month / 3 months, or a
custom range):
- Time per life area (actual logged + planned + utilization).
- Pace — logged vs estimate (beat / on / over).
- Deadline adherence — on-time vs late, average lateness, worst offenders.
- Completed and missed goals.
- Team delegation history and a daily/weekly effort trend.

**Task history** — the raw ledger.
- Defaults to the **last 7 days**; set a **From / To** range and click **Fetch**
  to load any window.
- Columns: Task, Area, Status, linked Goal, Created, Completed, Logged, Estimate,
  Deadline.
- Search and filter (All / Completed / Open) the loaded set.
- **↩ Reverse** any completed task to pull it back into your active list.

## Settings

- **Daily routine** — sleep / commute / work / working days (drives available
  time).
- **Ventures** — your life areas and their function tracks.
- **Wallpaper** — pick a photo or gradient; optional timed **shuffle** with a
  cross-fade and an adjustable interval.
- **Password** — set a new one.
- **Google accounts** — connect / manage calendar sync.

---

## Tips

- **Estimate everything.** The budget and overflow detection are only as good as
  your estimates — and Reports → Pace shows you how to calibrate them.
- **Log interruptions in the moment.** That's where "lost" time actually hides.
- **On the live site, hard-refresh after an update** (the PWA caches the old
  bundle); on mobile, fully close and reopen the app.
