Proposed behaviour: rail sits on the left, glass like everything else. Rests collapsed (74px, icons only) and expands to 230px on hover, showing labels; a ‹ toggle pins it either way (saved to localStorage). Active item gets a tinted pill. User avatar + logout move to the bottom of the rail. On narrow/mobile widths it falls back to a hamburger that slides the rail in as an overlay — so the bottom tray goes away entirely. Net win on PC: reclaims the ~64px the bottom tray ate, so you scroll less to browse tasks.
